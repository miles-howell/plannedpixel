Welcome to my corner of the web!

Hi, I'm Miles. I like working on low-level problems, mostly in AI and cybersecurity.

In high school I wrote a single-neuron network and reworked Karpathy's famous min-char-rnn to show off to classmates. I also co-led the programming club and consulted on my state's first high-school cybersecurity curriculum.

In college I spent two years on computer science and math, and started the ML/AI Special Interest Group. At the time there were no undergraduate ML courses, so we broke the topics down in plain English, with no high-level libraries. The goal wasn't to build anything. It was to understand the technology at its core.

"High-level libraries outsource your understanding!"

These days I run Planned Pixel Studio, where I take apart systems nobody fully understands anymore: legacy replacements and security testing. On my own time I experiment at the edges of AI research, like agents that learn to share profit. I miss the days when anything was possible with AI, and I think there are far more interesting, world-altering things to do with it than chatbots.

Want to chat about any of it? Email me at miles@plannedpixel.com.
