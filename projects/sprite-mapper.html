Sprite-Mapper

Upload a sprite sheet, click a tile, get its coordinates. No more counting rows and columns by hand.

A tiny single-page tool for anyone who has squinted at a sprite sheet counting tiles. Upload the sheet, enter the tile size and any margin between tiles, and click: you get the tile’s x and y coordinates instantly, with the selected tile outlined in red.

It’s one HTML file. No install, no build step.
