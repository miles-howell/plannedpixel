Tech Trio 3 HQ

A shared command center for three founders who each run their own software company, plus Spire, a virtual office building you can actually wander through.

access:
private · collab
year:
2026
built with:
Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, PWA

Three founders, three separate software companies, one shared office. Tech Trio 3 HQ is an installable web app where we keep shared priorities, a joint project board, open items, decisions, notes and contacts in one place.

It also has Spire, a play-first virtual office building: a lobby, an elevator, a café, an arcade floor with a snake game, dorms and a roof, plus an avatar studio for building your character.

~ highlights ~

  • Invite-only magic-link sign-in
  • A project board that moves ideas from Idea to Active to Parked
  • Scan a business card with your phone to add a shared contact
  • Installs as an app on a phone or desktop

This is a private repo I share with my partners. Ask me about it.
