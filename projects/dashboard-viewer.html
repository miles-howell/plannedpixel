Dashboard Viewer

A desktop launcher for department dashboards. Pick a department to open its PowerPoint dashboard, or upload a new version from the same screen.

built with:
Python, CustomTkinter, PyInstaller

A small desktop app for offices that share their dashboards as PowerPoint files. Pick a department and its dashboard opens, or upload a new version from the same screen. It greets first-time users with a splash screen, can point at a local folder or a shared network drive, and packages into a single .exe with PyInstaller for easy rollout.

The public version is a safe demo with generic departments and sample files.
