Privacy Policy

This is a static site with no accounts, no forms and no ads. Here is everything it collects, and why.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Who runs this site

plannedpixel.com is run by Miles Howell, trading as Planned Pixel Studio (“I”, “me”). If you have a question about this policy or your data, email miles@plannedpixel.com.

The short version

  • I use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. It sets cookies.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers.
  • The host that serves the site keeps standard request logs.
  • If you email me, I have your email. That’s the only way you can send me anything.
  • I don’t sell your data, run ads, or ask you to sign up for anything.

Analytics (Google Analytics)

Every page loads Google Analytics 4 (gtag.js, property G-6P5LRT54KQ). It tells me things like which pages were viewed, how visitors arrived (the referring site or search), roughly where they are (country or city, derived from the IP address), what kind of device, browser and screen they use, and how they interact with a page, such as scrolling or clicking an outbound link.

To tell one visit from the next, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies on plannedpixel.com, usually _ga and _ga_6P5LRT54KQ, which by default last up to two years. They hold a random identifier, not your name or email. According to Google, Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Google processes this data under its own terms; see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

I use these reports only to understand which projects people find interesting and where traffic comes from. You can opt out by blocking cookies for this site, using a content blocker, or installing Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The site works the same either way.

Web fonts (Google Fonts)

The Inter and Silkscreen fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). To download them, your browser sends Google its IP address and standard request headers, such as the user agent. Google Fonts doesn’t set cookies. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Hosting and server logs

Like every website, this one is served by a hosting provider that records basic information about each request, such as IP address, date and time, the page or file requested, referrer and user agent. These logs are used for security and to keep the site running, and are kept under the provider’s own retention policy. I don’t combine them with anything else.

What the site’s own code does

The site’s own JavaScript (js/site.js and js/redacted.js) only shows NEW badges, hover previews of project images, and the animated “redacted” stat. It doesn’t set cookies, use local storage, or send anything anywhere. The preview images come from this site, and are fetched ahead of time unless your browser has asked to save data.

Email

The contact links on this site open your own email app; there are no forms. If you email me, I receive your address, your message and anything you attach, and I use them to reply and, if we work together, to run the project. I don’t add you to a mailing list or share your message with anyone without your permission. I keep correspondence for as long as it’s useful, and I’ll delete it if you ask, unless I need to keep it for a legal or contractual reason.

Other sites

This site links to others: GitHub (including GitHub Sponsors), LinkedIn, project sites such as shrimple.dev, and my partners Makoons Technologies and Primagery. Once you follow a link you’re on their site, under their privacy policy, not this one.

Who I share data with

Only the services described above, which receive data as a direct result of you loading the site: Google (Analytics and Fonts) and the site’s hosting provider. I don’t sell or rent personal information, and I don’t share it for advertising. I would disclose information only if the law required it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the UK and EU, or under US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to ask what personal information I hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it’s used. Email me and I’ll help. In practice, the only personal information I can link to you is email you’ve sent me. Analytics data is tied to a random cookie ID, not to you, so I usually can’t find a specific visitor’s records; clearing your cookies starts a fresh ID. Where the GDPR applies, the basis for analytics is my legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used, and for email it is replying to you and any contract we make.

Children

This site isn’t aimed at children and I don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live).

Changes

If the site starts doing something new with data, I’ll update this page and the date at the top.
